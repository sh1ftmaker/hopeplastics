Family owned since 1964.

Don Borden started in plastics in 1946 and founded Hope Plastics in 1964 with his wife and two sons. Three machines in a single industrial unit grew to nearly twenty, and in 1974 the shop moved to its present building on Strohm Avenue in North Hollywood.

Today three generations of the family work together here, solving design, molding and manufacturing problems for customers in sporting goods, electronics, marine, pet, garden and consumer products. When you call, you talk to a Borden.
